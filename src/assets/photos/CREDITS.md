# Photo credits

Every photograph on the site is real (nothing is AI-generated). Photos come from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com),
uploaded between 2015 and 2021, before AI image generators existed. Unsplash's licence allows free commercial use
without attribution; the credits are listed here as a courtesy and so each image can be traced or replaced.

Files in this folder are picked up automatically by name (see `src/data/photos.js`). To swap a photo, replace the
file with another `.webp`/`.jpg`/`.png` of the same name.

## The Nigerian rule

The client requires that any person shown is a Black Nigerian. How each photo was chosen:

- **Photos of people** (`facility-vetting`, `step-employment`) were taken by photographers based in Nigeria (Lagos and
  elsewhere), and show Nigerian people.
- **One exception, kept at the site owner's request:** `step-guarantor` is a close-up of two people shaking hands
  (hands only, no faces). It was shot in Nairobi, Kenya, by a Nairobi-based studio, not in Nigeria. If the client
  objects, replace it with a Nigerian photo of the same kind.
- **Everything else is a place or an object**, not a person: the page heroes are Lagos and Abuja scenes shot by
  Lagos and Abuja photographers; the globe is a NASA satellite photograph.
- `step-address` is a street scene in Nigeria; people in it are passers-by, not subjects.

What this cannot prove: stock-photo metadata records where the photographer lives, not the nationality of the people
photographed. Have the client confirm each people photo before launch, or replace them with the company's own photos.
